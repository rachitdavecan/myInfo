## Song structure

| Section | What happens |
|---|---|
| Intro | Conch shell, damru roll, "Har Har Mahadev" shouts |
| Chorus | Shloka 1 (Na) — sung, chant-style |
| Verse 1 | Rap — Shiva's form: Kailash, snake, Ganga, ash, third eye |
| Chant | Shloka 2 (Ma) |
| Verse 2 | Rap — stories: Daksha yagya, Neelkanth, Nandi, Tandav |
| Chant | Shlokas 3 & 4 (Shi, Va) |
| Verse 3 | Rap — meaning of the five syllables / five elements |
| Chant | Shloka 5 (Ya) |
| Final chorus | Shloka 1 again with full choir |
| Outro | Phalashruti, slow and calm |
