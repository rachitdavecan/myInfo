# Om Namah Shivaya: AI video prompt pack

Scene-by-scene prompts for **Google Gemini / Veo**, **Kling**, **Runway** or **Luma**, matched to the song's sections.
Make each clip, download it, and send them all back. I'll cut them to the beat (103 BPM), add the lyrics and
transitions, and blend them with the vector animation.

## Settings for every clip
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 for YouTube. Make a 9:16 copy of your favourites for Reels/Shorts.
- **Length:** 5–8 s per clip.
- **Style suffix** (add to the end of every prompt):
  `cinematic, ultra detailed, volumetric light, deep blue and gold colour palette, slow smooth camera motion, devotional, majestic, 4K, no text, no watermark`
- **Avoid** (negative prompt, where the tool has one):
  `text, letters, watermark, logo, extra limbs, distorted face, deformed hands, cartoonish, low quality, flicker`
- **Depiction:** keep Mahadev serene and respectful. Calm face, eyes closed in meditation, traditional attributes.
  Most tools also accept these as "a meditating ascetic deity with blue skin" if a prompt is refused.

## Tips
- **Gemini / Veo:** paste a prompt plus the style suffix. Veo 3 can also add ambient sound; turn that off or mute it, since I use our song.
- **Kling / Runway:** use *image-to-video* for a consistent look. First make one strong still of Shiva (scene 3) with an
  image model, then animate that same image for scenes 3, 8, 14, 18 and 19 so he looks the same in every clip.
- Make 2 versions of the key shots (3, 8, 18, 19) and keep the best.
- Name the files by scene number (e.g. `scene08.mp4`) so I can place them automatically.
